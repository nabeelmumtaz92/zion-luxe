# ZionLuxe — First Traffic Plan (document only; nothing sent or posted)

Goal: first qualified inquiries, in priority order. All outreach is manual and by Nabeel.

1. **Past Zion Luxe contacts (2020–2022).** Warmest list. Personal message, short: you are relaunching as a personal luxury travel service; link to the page. Reply within 5 minutes when someone answers.
2. **Personal network and referrals.** Ask 10 people who travel well to forward the link to anyone planning a honeymoon, anniversary or milestone trip.
3. **Instagram.** Sample itineraries (use the three on the site), premium-cabin fare finds, short "how a proposal works" posts. Link in bio goes to the form. No invented client stories.
4. **Google Business Profile.** Create for Zion Luxe, LLC, St. Louis. Category: travel agency. Add the site, phone, and real photos once available.
5. **St. Louis wedding and event partners.** Planners, photographers, venues, bridal shops: offer honeymoon planning for their couples; discuss any referral terms plainly and in writing.

## Working the leads
- Respond to every inquiry in under an hour when possible (WhatsApp or call first).
- Most high-ticket trips book on the 3rd–5th contact. Use the `Next follow-up` column.
- Statuses: New → Contacted → Proposal sent → Booked → Traveled → Not a fit / No response.
- Do not promise outcomes, prices or availability.
